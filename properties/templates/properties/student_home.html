How Rongo Student Homes Works

Follow these simple steps to find your accommodation.

Step 1

Find Your Accommodation

Start by browsing available accommodation around Rongo University and discover properties that match your needs.

Step 2

Browse Verified Properties

Explore accommodation listings that have been reviewed and approved on Rongo Homes.

Step 3

Check the Location

Check the area, nearby landmarks and distance from Rongo University before choosing a property.

Step 4

Check Rooms & Amenities

Review available rooms, monthly pricing and the amenities offered by the property.

Step 5

View Property Details

Open a property to explore its available accommodation information before deciding.

Step 6

Compare Your Options

Save and compare properties so you can make a more informed accommodation decision.

Step 7

Unlock Property Contacts

When you are ready to contact a property, follow the secure contact-unlock process. Successful payment gives you access to the available contact details.

Step 8

Find Your Home With Confidence
